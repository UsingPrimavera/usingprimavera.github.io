# How-To Guides

How-To Guides are **task-oriented**. They answer the question "how do I…?" for a practitioner who already knows what they want to achieve.

Each guide is a sequence of steps to complete a specific task. They assume you know your way around the product.

**Products covered:** Oracle Primavera P6, Oracle Primavera Unifier, Oracle Analytics Server, BI Publisher, Power Query
